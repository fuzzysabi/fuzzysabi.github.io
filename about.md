Wabi-Sabi: The appreciation of the imperfect, impermanent, and incomplete. 
>"Wabi-sabi nurtures all that is authentic by acknowledging three simple 
>realities: nothing lasts, nothing is finished, and nothing is perfect."
>
>-Richard Powell

Fuzzy-Sabi: Someone who continually tinkers, makes, and improves things which 
are imperfect, impermanent, or incomplete, and appreciates this
journey of craftsmanship and learning
