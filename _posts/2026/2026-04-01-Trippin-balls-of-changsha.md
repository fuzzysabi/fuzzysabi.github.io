Instead of bollards, the concrete-in-a-pipe structure we often see in front of
buildings to prevent vehicles from being where they shouldn't be, Chang Sha has
marble balls in several sizes, lining the streets and sidewalks at ankle height.
There were thousands of them, from little tiny ones you hadn't noticed until
you're looking at the sky, to huge ones that have you doubling over in pain on a
stone yoga ball.
